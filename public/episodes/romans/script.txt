Ten weird things you didn't know about the ancient Romans.

Ten. They ate dinner lying down. On couches. Forks? Nope. Fingers.

Nine. Fast food was already a thing. Pompeii had street counters selling hot food to go.

Eight. Their ketchup? Rotten fish guts. It was called garum, and they put it on everything.

Seven. Fancy party snack: dormice. Fattened up inside clay jars. [sarcastic] Yum.

Six. No soap. They rubbed olive oil on their skin... and scraped the dirt off with a metal stick.

Five. Public toilets had no walls. Just one long bench of holes... and your neighbors.

Four. Toilet paper? A sponge on a stick. And it was probably shared.

Three. Laundry was washed in pee. Workers stomped the clothes in big tubs of it.

Two. The emperor Vespasian put a tax on that pee. His excuse? Money doesn't stink.

And number one. Some Romans whitened their teeth... [whispers] with pee. A poet even roasted a guy for it.
